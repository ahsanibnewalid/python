# University Connect - Social Creator & Dual Profile Update

Based on the supplied V9 Stories Fix baseline.

## Included
- Text-only posts (no media required)
- Photo posts
- Dedicated Reel video posts
- Story photo/video uploads with 24-hour expiry
- Clickable post authors to public profiles
- Facebook-style cover photo upload
- Automatic feed post when profile or cover photo changes
- Public profile format selection: Facebook Detailed or Premium Professional CV
- Premium professional CV layout oriented toward Bangladesh job applications
- Existing messaging and V9 functionality preserved
- Additive users-table migrations for `cover_photo` and `profile_view`

## Important
Keep the existing `database.db`, `static/uploads`, and `private_media` folders when replacing the application files.
